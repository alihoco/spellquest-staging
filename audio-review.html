Spellmaster: Audio review

Five draft Year 5–6 words. Listen to complete-word pronunciation, definitions, examples and spelling explanations before specialist sign-off.

Review-only drafts. This is not a validated lesson or a test for children. No unreviewed sound chunks are played. The voice is AI-generated, and pronunciation and teaching scripts still need human approval.

Reviewer checklist

Listen for pronunciation accuracy, clear natural British English, comfortable pacing, correct definition and context, and teaching that does not confuse written chunks with spoken sounds.

To approve for use with children, each item also requires specialist review of phoneme–grapheme mapping and any word-specific teaching claims. This page does not change approval status.
