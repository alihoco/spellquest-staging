INVITATION-ONLY TEST

Information for parents and guardians

Help improve Spellmaster without sharing a child's identity.

Adults must set up and supervise the account. Please do not ask a child to enter personal information. This private test is not a school assessment or clinical test.

What we collect

The adult chooses a username and password. For the child, the adult supplies only a made-up nickname, age in completed years, and a chosen avatar. The system stores spelling attempts, word-group preferences, progress and assessment outcomes in our private Render PostgreSQL database hosted in Frankfurt, Germany. There is no need for a real name, exact date of birth, email address, school, telephone number or diagnosis. Please do not put those details into nicknames or feedback.

How we use it

To deliver voice-led spelling practice, remember progress and adapt lessons to spelling performance. Age changes interface appearance and tone; it does not decide spelling skill. We do not compare children with each other, advertise to them, or create public rankings.

Research is separate

Children are not enrolled in formal spelling research by using the learning pilot. Their outcomes are not included in the adult grouping research analysis. Any future child research would require further guardian authorisation, appropriate learner assent, reviewed study procedures and independent safeguards.

Access, safety and privacy

Only adults with a private invitation code may create a guardian account. The code is an access restriction, not proof of parental responsibility; we therefore ask the invited adult to expressly confirm their authority. The responsible adult is in charge of signing in, selecting a learner and signing out after use. The site link alone does not protect information. Please use a private browser/device where possible and never share your password or recovery code.

Your choices

Participation is voluntary and free. You can stop testing, export the account's information or delete the child profile and account from Privacy & Account Settings. Deleted application records may temporarily remain within hosting backups according to provider retention. You can contact the person who invited you about access or deletion problems. There is no payment collection and test profiles will not be automatically charged.

Important limitations

Spellmaster is an experimental educational product, not a diagnosis, a substitute for individual teacher support, or a proven dyslexia treatment. Visual letter groups are optional memory suggestions and are not necessarily spoken syllables. A parent should help a child if anything feels confusing or uncomfortable.
