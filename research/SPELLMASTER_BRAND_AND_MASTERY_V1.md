# Spellmaster — Brand and Personal Mastery System v1.0

**Decision date:** 11 November 2026  
**Product name:** Spellmaster (replaces SpellQuest in new product-facing development)  
**Domains intended:** spellmaster.com and spellmaster.co.uk (user-reported availability; registration and rights not independently confirmed).  
**Original project's GitHub and staging paths:** maintained until migration to avoid breaking active links and tests.

## Product promise

**Better today than yesterday. Your words, your journey, your mastery.**

Spellmaster is an evidence-informed, audio-accessible spelling-learning system focused on children with dyslexia and other learners who struggle with spelling, with age-respectful experiences for ages 4–16 and adults.

### Three permanently separate dimensions

1. **Presentation age:** month/year of birth (optional and privacy-minimised), learner-chosen world and narration style. No connection to spelling ability.
2. **Response accessibility:** suitable tiles, complete on-screen alphabet, keyboard and spoken guidance; controls are learner-selectable.
3. **Actual spelling mastery:** explicit teaching, independent retrieval, delayed retention and transfer. Never inferred from birth year, game score or peer comparison.

## A mastery architecture without finish-line shame

Every learner may become a **Master** of a *specific taught skill or pattern* at their current stage, including early letter–sound mappings. New skills unlock new mastery paths; existing masters retain earlier accomplishments, subject to helpful low-pressure refresher practice.

**Example progression within each skill realm (provisional product design, not a research-backed taxonomy):**
- **Discoverer:** Encounters the concept and hears the correct model.
- **Builder:** Completes supported construction and shows understanding of the component.
- **Independent Speller:** Produces the target correctly unaided with accessible input.
- **Master:** Recalls independently after a meaningful delay and succeeds across appropriate examples.
- **Enduring Master:** Demonstrates retention at later intervals and relevant transfer, with uncertainty and age-appropriate accessibility respected.

For every new realm, a learner can begin again as a Discoverer without losing their Master status in older realms. Example realms: early sounds, digraphs, tricky vowel patterns, doubling, prefixes and suffixes, meaningful word families, advanced multisyllabic spelling. Avoid making realm names correspond to school-year rank.

## Reward integrity

- **Master** is earned through *demonstrated learning*, not points, consecutive-day streaks or lucky multiple-choice choices.
- Displayed letters / recognition tasks are explicitly marked as supported practice, not independent spelling.
- Delayed independent recall and appropriate transfer support robust mastery claims; thresholds and review intervals must be tested and refined.
- Celebrate personal improvement and effort with story progress, coins, shields, trophies, unlocks or baddie challenges as optional fun.
- Never subtract previously earned trophies for forgotten words or punish missed days. Offer friendly refresh quests.
- A learner can feel successful at *any* development stage. No global leaderboards, class comparisons, age-norm rankings or pejorative titles.
- Don't reward speed at the expense of accuracy, or make game currency pressure the learner into continuing.
- Use mature visual skins and reward language for teens and adults: e.g. challenge archives, mastery maps, missions, personal bests.

## Learner-facing example

'You've become a Master of **sh** spellings! You remembered them on another day without clues. Your next adventure opens a new word pattern, and you can always revisit your earlier achievements.'

## Core teaching remains unchanged

HEAR → DISCOVER → UNDERSTAND → EXPLAIN → BUILD → RECALL → REINFORCE → REMEMBER.

New mastery badges are a presentation of *evidence-backed learner records*. They are **not** a replacement for learning or a new curricular outcome. Adaptive instruction and the proposed A/B/C studies remain research-gated, and unreviewed phonetic sound samples must never be automatically published.

## Release and migration checklist

- Use Spellmaster for all new product copy, curriculum documentation and future UI.
- Keep existing GitHub repo and staging URL stable while prototyping. Migrate intentionally with redirects and retained history.
- Verify domain registration, relevant trademarks and potential brand confusion before commercial launch.
- Specify badge evidence policy and delayed retention thresholds with specialist review.
- Separate reward points/coins from true, persisted mastery classifications.
- Preserve saved learner achievements across future theme changes.
- Test trophy wording for confidence, emotional safety and age sensitivity.
