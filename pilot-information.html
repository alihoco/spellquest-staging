Volunteer trial information

Please read this before creating a Spellmaster test account.

Who can join?

Only invited adults aged 18 or over. The private account trial is not open to children and must not contain a child's name, date of birth, neurodiversity information, health information or other sensitive details. Parents who want to observe spelling lessons with a child may instead use the separate browser-only demonstration without a child account.

What is being tested?

We are testing the clarity of the onboarding experience, the narrator, the ten-word starting assessment, spelling practice, personal progress and account recovery. We want usability feedback, not exam results.

What is saved?

If you create an invited adult account, our private Render PostgreSQL database in Frankfurt, Germany stores your chosen username, password hash, recovery-code hash, avatar selection, optional presentation preferences, dates of account use, assessment results, spelling attempts and revision history. We do not request a real name, email address, payment information or diagnoses for this test. Session credentials are held in your browser tab while signed in.

Who can access it?

Authorised Spellmaster project administrators and its hosting providers only as needed to run, troubleshoot and protect the trial. The spelling voice service generates prepared word prompts rather than requiring your personal profile details. Test data is not sold or used for advertising or peer comparisons.

Cost and trial

Participation is free. No card details are collected. Trial accounts will not be charged or automatically converted into paid subscriptions. The proposed future £10/year individual membership is not yet billed. Features may change or be reset during testing.

Your choices and support

You can sign in and use the Privacy & Account Settings page to export the information held in the account or permanently remove it. Data stored in database backups may remain temporarily according to the hosting provider's backup retention. To ask a question, report a privacy concern or obtain an account-access review, contact the Spellmaster volunteer coordinator using the invitation channel through which you received this test. Do not include passwords or recovery codes in messages.

Important limits

This is an invitation-only technical pilot, not a validated clinical, diagnostic or educational assessment. Spellmaster does not diagnose dyslexia or any other condition. The ten words are an informal starting observation and your learning path can change as you practise.
