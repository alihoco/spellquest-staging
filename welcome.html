Sign in to Spellmaster

In the finished service, adult account holders and school staff will sign in securely. Children will use their school-assigned or guardian-managed learner access, without needing a personal email address.

Where a learner's progress will live

Once secure accounts are connected, Spellmaster will keep a learner's starting point, independent attempts, spelling-pattern difficulties, chosen teaching supports and delayed recall history — even when they switch devices or classes.

The current teaching pilot remembers progress only in this browser. The teacher report is illustrative, not real pupil data.
